***6:05 - 6:15 - Explain "Photosynthesis"***



**6:06 - pg 2**



Photosynthesis is a plant-based animated musical comedy drama set in a world inhabited by photogenic plants. The feature follows a cilantro who owns a salon named Rau Ram (“cilantro” in Vietnamese) as she must earn her green card or else she gets uprooted and loses custody of her son Parsley. The series is a sequel to the feature and focuses more on the kids, Parsley and Pyrus, a good-natured herb and free-spirited pear, and their pet bee Bombus as they navigate Dirty City and create chaos with their naive antics and innocent friendship.



Photosynthesis is adapted from my stage musical *Turnover: A New Leaf*, which Queer Theatre Kalamazoo had a world premiere run from May 16-18 and concert June 7-8. It builds the world from just a handful of characters.



**6:08 - pg 3**



Photosynthesis is set in the garden nation of Woodwork. Inspired by Michigan and American continents, Woodwork has two peninsulas, shaped like a leaf above a tree trunk. The leaf peninsula has towns like Mt. Woodwork. The trunk has cities like Dirty City and the capitol. The story is mainly in Salontro salon or FigLeaf nightclub in Dirty City. Buildings are often shaped like pots or trees. Paddy Kingdom is inspired by Vietnam and Afro-Eurasian continents.

**6:10 - pg 11**



The objective of each animated series episode is to a) introduce new plant characters; b) connect biodiversity with themes of family; c) explore Woodwork and Paddy Kingdom; and d) take photos. The characters can take selfies and photos that will be planted into the end credits of each episode. The photos may also be significant in feature episodes. Every time the characters take a photo, they sing the photosynthesis jingle: “Photosynthesis, photosynthesis, photo!” There will be at least a song written by me or another songwriter in each episode.
